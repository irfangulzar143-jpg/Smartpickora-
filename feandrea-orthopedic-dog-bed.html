Features to consider

Supportive foam base

The egg-crate texture is designed to distribute pressure across the resting surface. Comfort varies by dog, so it should not be treated as a medical product.

Raised padded sides

The U-shaped bolsters give dogs a raised edge to rest their head against, with a lower front entry for easier access.

Water-resistant surface

The treated surface may help with small spills, but water-resistant does not mean fully waterproof. Clean spills promptly.

Cleaning & unpacking

  • Remove the base foam before washing the cover.
  • Wash the cover at a maximum of 40°C, following the product label.
  • The foam itself is not washable; keep it dry.
  • Side bolsters are not removable.
  • If vacuum-packed, allow 24–48 hours for the bed to expand fully after unpacking.
Before you buy: Measure your dog while lying down and compare those measurements with the usable inner sleeping area—not only the outside dimensions. Confirm the exact size, colour, shipping, returns and current price on Amazon.
