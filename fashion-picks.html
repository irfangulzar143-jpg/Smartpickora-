What to verify before adding a product to SmartPickora

Use this checklist when a specific item is selected. A search phrase alone is not enough to publish a product recommendation.

1. Exact product identity

Product title, brand, ASIN or stable listing URL, seller and selected variation.

2. Fit & size chart

Use the specific brand/listing chart. Record inches or centimeters and note whether it measures the body or garment.

3. Materials & care

Verify fabric composition, lining, closure, country of origin where listed, and care instructions.

4. Current price & stock

Check the selected color and size at the time of publication. Avoid hard-coded prices that may become stale.

5. Returns & delivery

Check shipping, return conditions, coupon requirements and taxes for the shopper's location.

6. Editorial value

Explain who it may suit, what to compare and any limitations. Don't claim hands-on testing unless it happened.
